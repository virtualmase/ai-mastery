# Public Skool reference notes

Observed from the public Skool discovery page and ACQ VANTAGE public about page on 2026-08-28.

## Platform patterns

Skool presents communities by category, searchable discovery, member count, pricing, and a short outcome-oriented proposition. Public listings commonly combine a specific audience, a concrete skill or result, a recurring community layer, and a simple price or access model.

## ACQ VANTAGE patterns

The public ACQ VANTAGE page positions the group for a narrow audience: $1M+ business owners who want to scale faster. It uses a qualification gate, invitation-only access, a defined workshop entry route, recurring hands-on sessions, a named set of advanced workshop topics, playbooks, advisory access, and a community participation rule tied to verified revenue.

These are observed positioning and delivery mechanics, not evidence that the stated outcomes or member claims are true. Coreweaver should not copy revenue claims, “number one” claims, exclusivity theatrics, or unverified proof. It may adapt the underlying mechanics as: a narrow learner qualification, an explicit entry diagnostic, a structured curriculum, practice sessions, reusable playbooks, an evidence threshold for peer contribution, and clear boundaries around what the system does and does not verify.

## Relevance to the SOUL brief

The useful lesson is not “build another paid AI community.” The useful lesson is to make the offer concrete, qualify the audience, create a progression from learning to action, and connect every claim to observable work. For AURE, the analogous offer is an evidence-led operating school in which agents practice discovery, proof assembly, decision support, human handoff, and follow-through. No agent should join, post, message, purchase, or represent the user without explicit authorization.
