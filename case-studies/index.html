Applied work · Evidence before outcome

Show the work. Keep the unknowns.

Field studies record decisions, implementation evidence, boundaries, and next checks without turning a documented direction into a success claim.

Collection boundary

Working records, not endorsements.

These first-party records do not certify a client, product, system, strategy, or result. Each page names the evidence it inspected and the outcomes it did not establish.

Publication standard

A claim earns its class.

Every material statement is handled as one of four things. This keeps observations from quietly becoming promises and makes correction possible when the underlying record changes.

FactDirectly supported by an inspected source.
InterpretationA bounded reading of the available evidence.
FrameworkA reusable method, not a guaranteed result.
UnknownHeld open until attributable evidence exists.
