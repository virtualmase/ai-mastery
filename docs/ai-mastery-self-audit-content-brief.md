# AI Mastery Self-Audit: Content Brief

## Audience and question

This page is for readers, potential collaborators, and future maintainers who need to decide what AI Mastery has publicly implemented, what can be inspected directly, and which outcome claims are deliberately absent.

## Page job and unique contribution

The page documents an internal self-audit of a public research site. Its contribution is a reusable method: distinguish implementation evidence from audience or performance outcomes, publish the limitation alongside the evidence, and provide a correction route. It is useful without requiring a reader to trust a promotional summary.

## Claim boundary

The page may state public repository, deployment, route, discovery-file, and source-link facts recorded on 2026-08-27. It must label interpretation and framework statements. It must not claim rankings, citation, traffic, revenue, learner outcomes, security, accessibility, legal compliance, product availability, or independent verification.

## Information architecture

The page leads with the audit boundary, then presents implementation observations, unknown outcomes, method, release record, sources, correction route, and maintenance triggers. Its single next action is to inspect the evidence ledger or raise a specific correction through the public repository issue form.

## Assets and distribution

The page uses only a semantic CSS relationship diagram and no generated or documentary media. It has no distribution derivative: the page’s job is accountable self-documentation, not social promotion.

## Ownership and release

The public site identifies Mason Nguyen as its creator. This page was drafted with AI assistance from the linked first-party repository and live-site sources; it is not an independent audit. A material route, curriculum, discovery-file, or publication change triggers recheck. Release requires the self-audit validator and a human review of any proposed outcome statement.
