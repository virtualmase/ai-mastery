GEO pathway · foundation 06

Measure the system you built.

Crawlability is a condition. A ranking is an outcome. A citation is an observation. An answer is a context-dependent event. This lesson teaches you not to collapse those four things into one optimistic dashboard.

Learning boundary. This lesson teaches an evaluation model. It does not guarantee rankings, citations, retrieval, conversion, or inclusion in any search or answer system.
Learning objective

Separate four kinds of evidence.

By the end, you should be able to build a measurement plan that distinguishes structural checks, platform metrics, independent observations, and unknown outcomes. You should also be able to state what a passing technical audit proves—and what it leaves unanswered.

01 · Structural health

Can the system be found and read?

Check HTTP status, canonical URLs, robots directives, sitemap parity, ordinary internal links, page metadata, structured data syntax, and image attributes. These are inspectable properties of the published surface.

02 · Search performance

What happened in a search platform?

Use first-party reporting such as Search Console to inspect impressions, clicks, queries, pages, countries, and indexing or enhancement reports. Record the property, date range, filters, and comparison before interpreting a change.

03 · Answer observation

What did an answer system actually show?

Capture the question, date, locale, device, model or search surface, exact response, linked sources, and whether the response changed. A single output is an observation, not a stable visibility score.

04 · Unknown boundary

What cannot be concluded yet?

Do not infer Domain Authority, ranking improvement, citation growth, traffic, or answer inclusion from a local route audit. Mark those outcomes unknown until the appropriate independent measurement exists.

Practice lab · evidence before confidence

Evaluate a release without overclaiming.

A team publishes a 52-page learning site. Every route returns HTTP 200. The sitemap matches the route manifest. Internal links are crawlable and no page is more than two clicks from home. The team has not yet connected Search Console, collected a backlink baseline, or run a documented answer-observation study.

  1. List the claims that the technical audit supports.
  2. List the claims that remain unknown.
  3. Choose one first-party measurement source and define its date range.
  4. Write a recheck record that another person could reproduce.

Answer rationale

The audit supports a bounded statement: the published route graph is reachable and its inspected crawl controls are internally consistent. It does not support a statement that the site ranks, earns citations, has Domain Authority, receives traffic, or appears in an AI answer. The next safe measurement is to verify the property in Search Console, inspect indexing and performance reports, and record a baseline. A separate off-site authority baseline and a documented answer-observation protocol may be useful, but neither should be represented as a standard Google metric.
