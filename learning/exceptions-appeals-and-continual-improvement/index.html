Foundation 04 · Exceptions and improvement

Make repair
visible.

A governed workflow is not defined only by its happy path. When policy, evidence, affected-party experience, or system behavior disagrees, the next safe state must be explicit, owned, reviewable, and capable of producing a correction.

STARTING QUESTIONWhat happens when the policy or its result is disputed?

Design a hold, resolver, appeal record, corrective action, and recheck trigger.

Learning boundary. This lesson is an exception and improvement exercise, not legal advice, a rights determination, a fairness guarantee, a safety assessment, or a promise that an appeal path will produce a particular result. A correction record shows a response; it does not prove the original decision was harmless.

Learning objective

Turn disagreement into accountable work.

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to define safe holds, human escalation, appeal records, corrective actions, and the evidence required to re-open or retire a policy.

01 · Hold

What must pause?

Name the action, downstream effect, expiry, and condition that cannot proceed while evidence or authority is unresolved.

02 · Appeal

Who can challenge?

Record the affected party, channel, time window, evidence they may provide, and the person who must receive the challenge.

03 · Repair

What changes now?

Separate immediate containment, case correction, policy revision, and system change. Do not overwrite the original decision record.

04 · Recheck

When is it learned?

Define the review trigger, owner, sample, measure, and retirement condition so a closed exception does not silently disappear.

Practice lab

Design the safe hold.

A workflow flags a person’s request as outside policy. The evidence is incomplete, the requester disputes the classification, and the operator cannot tell whether the policy changed after the request was made.

  1. Name the action that must pause and the immediate consequence of holding it.
  2. Define the appeal channel, resolver, evidence packet, and response window.
  3. Separate case correction from policy or model change, preserving the original record.
  4. Write the recheck trigger that determines whether the policy needs revision or retirement.

Answer direction

Repair is a first-class state.

Make the exception legible to the person affected and the operator responsible. Do not treat a resolved ticket as evidence that the policy, signal, or outcome was correct.
