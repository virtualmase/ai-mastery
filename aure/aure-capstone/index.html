AURE Capstone: The Trusted Buyer Path

Connect all sixteen artifacts into one reviewable path from buyer question to evidence, decision, handoff, and maintained truth.

Working artifact

operating packet

AURE-16 produces the integrative operating packet. It is a reviewable implementation, not a certification or guarantee of business results.

  1. Name the actor and the decision.
  2. Record the evidence source and observation date.
  3. Set the authority boundary and unresolved question.
  4. Choose the next review event. Missing evidence stays unknown.
